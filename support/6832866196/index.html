Common questions

How do I scan a receipt?

Open the scan option in the app, capture a clear, well-lit receipt and review the recognized items before saving. Recognition can make mistakes; check names, quantities and prices.

Why am I not receiving expiry reminders?

Enable reminders in the app and allow notifications in iOS Settings. Check each item's expiry date and your Focus and notification settings.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use Restore Purchases on the purchase screen or in the app's settings, using the Apple Account that made the purchase. An internet connection is required. Contact us if your purchase is still unavailable.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Open iOS Settings, tap your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. Select the app to view renewal information or cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles billing and refund requests through Report a Problem.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual food entries and shopping items within the app. Deleting the app removes its local data; manage device backups separately in your Apple settings. Contact us for privacy requests involving information sent to support or our services.

Why is a barcode not found?

Product databases do not contain every barcode. Check your connection, try another lookup source if available, or enter the food manually.
