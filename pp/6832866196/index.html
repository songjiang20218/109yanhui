Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Xi'an Xinghe Yunchuang Network Technology Co., Ltd. handles information in Food Inventory & Expiry Dates (App ID 6832866196).

Your food and receipt information

Food entries, quantities, expiry dates, shopping lists, spending records and selected images are stored on your device. Receipt text recognition uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. These user-entered inventory records and receipt text are not included in our analytics events. Device backups may contain local app data according to your Apple settings.

Permissions and product lookup

Camera and photo access are used when you choose to scan a barcode or receipt or attach an image. Notifications are optional and used for reminders. You can change permissions in iOS Settings. Online barcode lookup sends the scanned product code to the selected product-data provider, such as Open Food Facts, UPCitemdb or ApiZero; that provider also receives ordinary network information, such as your IP address. Availability depends on the app's configuration.

Subscriptions, analytics and attribution

Apple processes payments; we do not receive your full payment-card details. Apple and DingYue services process product, transaction and entitlement information to provide and restore purchases. The app integrates DingYue, Google Firebase Analytics, Adjust and Meta App Events for app functionality, usage measurement and marketing attribution. These services may process app or device identifiers, app interactions, purchase events, timestamps, device and system information and network information. Tracking that requires App Tracking Transparency permission is subject to your iOS authorization. Declining tracking does not prevent you from managing your food inventory.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to operate features, verify subscriptions, measure usage and campaign performance, troubleshoot problems and answer support requests. Service providers process relevant information for these purposes under their own applicable privacy terms. We do not sell your personal information. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect users and the service. Providers may process information in countries other than your own.

Retention, security and choices

You can delete entries within the app and remove local app data by deleting the app. Copies in device backups are controlled through your Apple settings. Purchase, analytics and attribution records are retained by the relevant providers according to their purposes, settings and policies. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information we hold, or exercise other rights available under applicable law. We may need information to verify your request.

Support and children

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide, and use it to answer your request. Please avoid sending sensitive receipt or payment information. The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
